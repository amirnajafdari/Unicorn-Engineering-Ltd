Our Story

About Unicorn Engineering Ltd

A mechanical fabrication company based in Ellesmere Port, manufacturing metal structures and parts of structures since 1982.

Who We Are

Structural Steel & Metal Fabrication, In-House

Unicorn Engineering Ltd was incorporated on 15 April 1982 for the manufacture of metal structures and parts of structures. Since then, we've built a reputation for precision fabrication of structural steelwork and bespoke metal components for industrial and commercial clients.

Every project is fabricated in-house at our Park Works site — from cutting and welding through to finishing and site installation — giving clients a single point of contact from initial drawing to final delivery.

We work in mild steel, stainless steel and a range of structural sections, manufacturing everything from individual parts of structures to complete steel frameworks.
