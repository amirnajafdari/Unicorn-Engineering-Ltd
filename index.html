Who We Are

Metal Fabrication Since 1982

Unicorn Engineering Ltd was incorporated on 15 April 1982 for the manufacture of metal structures and parts of structures. Since then, we've fabricated structural steelwork and bespoke metal components from our Park Works site in Ellesmere Port.

Our in-house team handles every stage — from cutting and welding through to finishing and site installation — giving clients a single point of contact from drawing to delivery.
