What We Offer

Our Services

Structural steel fabrication and manufacture of metal structures and parts of structures — from design through to site installation.

01
Design & Manufacture

Structural Steel Fabrication

We design, cut, weld and assemble structural steelwork in-house at our Park Works site — from individual beams and columns through to complete steel frameworks for industrial and commercial buildings.

Every job is manufactured to the relevant engineering drawings and specifications, with material traceability and quality checks carried out throughout the fabrication process.

  • Structural steel beams, columns & frameworks
  • Bandsaw cutting & forming
  • MIG / TIG welding & assembly
  • Drilling, machining & finishing
  • Delivery & site installation
Structural Steel Fabrication
02
Frameworks & Platforms

Metal Structures & Frameworks

We manufacture bespoke structural frameworks, platforms, walkways, stairs and support structures to your specification — engineered for industrial process plant, warehousing and commercial sites.

From concept through to fabricated steelwork, our team works closely with contractors and engineers to deliver structures that fit first time.

  • Platforms, walkways & access stairs
  • Equipment support structures
  • Mezzanine & structural frameworks
  • Handrail & guarding fabrication
  • Galvanising & painting coordination
Metal Structures & Frameworks
03
Welding & Assembly

Precision Welding & Fabrication

Our welders and fabricators bring together decades of experience to deliver precision welding and fabrication of metal components and assemblies, finished to exacting tolerances.

We work in mild steel, stainless steel and a range of structural sections, with every weld carried out to the relevant procedures and standards.

  • MIG, TIG & MMA welding
  • Mild & stainless steel fabrication
  • Sheet metal work
  • Assembly & sub-assembly
  • Quality inspection & finishing
Precision Welding & Fabrication
04
Parts & Components

Bespoke Parts & Components

We manufacture individual parts of structures and bespoke metal components to exact engineering drawings — supporting OEMs, contractors and maintenance teams with one-off and batch production.

From prototype parts to repeat-order components, our in-house capability means fast turnaround without compromising on accuracy.

  • Precision turning & milling
  • Bespoke brackets & fixings
  • Replacement & repair parts
  • Small batch & one-off production
  • Technical drawing review
Bespoke Parts & Components
05
Site & Material Handling

Workshop & Site Services

Alongside our fabrication work, we provide mechanical site services and general workshop support — from on-site mechanical fitting through to workshop-based repairs and modifications.

Our Park Works workshop is equipped with a 15-tonne capacity overhead crane, giving us the material handling capability to safely move and manoeuvre large, heavy fabrications during manufacture.

  • Mechanical site services
  • Workshop-based repairs & modifications
  • In-house turning & milling
  • Material handling — 15-tonne overhead crane
  • General engineering support
